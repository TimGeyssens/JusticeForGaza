About This Project

This project was born out of a refusal to remain silent. It is a decentralized effort to document the intersection of human rights, neurodiversity, and the global systems of exploitation that profit from war and ecocide. Because the truth is often targeted by censorship, this site is designed to be easily mirrored and republished by anyone, anywhere.

About the Creator

Today, I balance my technical background with a deep commitment to social care and education. I teach arts to graduate students from all different backgrounds and work at a Medical Pediatric Center. Despite my professional achievements, the current economic system forces me to work 140% just to survive, living month by month. I also run a small independent record label, Pool Needle, as a way to support culture outside of corporate control.

On a professional level, I have always followed my passion for technology and community. I have held several high-level corporate jobs, but I witnessed firsthand the "Bullshit Jobs" problem: the bloat of management and the extraction of profit by people who produce nothing while profiting from the work of others. I was honored as an Umbraco MVP for several years and became a well-known figure in the open-source world. I helped grow the then 3-man company to the dozens it employs now—though I saw it lose its soul as it became corporate.

I have never been a fan of hierarchy; I was never good at sports and was frequently bullied in primary school. These experiences, combined with a diagnosis of bipolar disorder at age 30 and childhood selective mutism, have given me a unique perspective on the "Neuro-typical" system. It has taken me 43 years to fully realize how corrupt and unfair this machine truly is. I have always been someone who questions the 'why'—why do we live? Why do some people have everything while others have nothing at all? We all know someone, or have a family member, who suffers from cancer or mental health issues. I have come to realize that these are not just individual tragedies; they are issues created and sustained by the system we live in.

I find immense strength in the great philosophers and often feel that the vast majority of people live in "the cave," watching shadows on the wall and never entering the light of raw reality. Music and dancing are my essential outlets—rhythms that bypass the system's noise. I have two children, and for them—and for all children in the world—I refuse to remain silent.

I dedicate this work to my parents, my grandparents, and my great-grandmother, who raised me with so much love and taught me the most valuable lesson of all: that you can be truly content with very little.

Censorship Resistance: How to Mirror

If this site is taken down or blocked, you can host your own copy in minutes. The site is built with simple HTML and CSS—no complex databases or servers are required.

1. Clone the Source Code

You can clone the entire project using Git:

git clone https://github.com/TimGeyssens/JusticeForGaza.git

2. Download as ZIP

Go to the GitHub repository, click the green "Code" button, and select "Download ZIP". Unzip the files on your computer.

3. Host it Anywhere

  • GitHub Pages: Upload the files to a new repository and enable "Pages" in the settings.
  • Netlify/Vercel: Simply drag and drop the folder into their web interfaces for free hosting.
  • Local Server: Run python3 -m http.server in the folder to view it locally.
