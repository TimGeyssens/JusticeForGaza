1. The Shame: A Child Must Save the Adults

It is the ultimate moral bankruptcy of our time: we leave the salvation of the planet and humanity to our children. While "normal" adults hide behind diplomatic mist, economic interests, and polite excuses, it is Greta Thunberg who, with her razor-sharp focus, forces the world to look in the mirror.

  • The Inverted World: We call her "extreme," but what is more extreme: a child calling for action, or a world leader who remains silent while the earth burns and Gaza is being slaughtered?
  • The Child as a Shield: The adults have failed. They are too busy counting their blood money to see the facts that a child has understood long ago.

2. Our "Superpower": Neurodiversity as a Weapon

What the system calls a limitation — autism, bipolarity, neurodiversity — is in reality our salvation. It is a filter that refuses to lie.

  • Black-and-White Clarity: Where those in power speak in "nuances" to gloss over genocide and ecocide, we see the raw facts. The truth is non-negotiable.
  • The "Madness" is Rational: In a world racing toward an abyss, the person pressing the brakes is the only one truly sane. Our vulnerability makes us tireless.

3. The System that Sickens and Kills Children

We live in a construction built on exploitation, led by the 1% who steal more and more while the rest must make do with less and less.

  • Mental Depletion: Here in the West, we make our children sick. We squeeze them into a performance system that breaks their spirits, solely to fill the pockets of the elite.
  • Physical Destruction: In Palestine, we allow children to be crushed under rubble, starved, and traumatized by an occupation machine funded by that same system.
  • The Unpunished Lie: Leaders at the top can lie directly about "self-defense" or "sustainability" without any consequence. Their impunity is the cancer of our society.

4. The Call for Justice: Free Palestine

Those who see the world as Greta does cannot look away from the horrors committed by the state of Israel. The struggle for the climate is inextricably linked to the liberation of the oppressed.

  • No Nuance for Genocide: The systematic slaughter of a people is not a "complex conflict." It is a crime against humanity.
  • One Struggle: Our fight for a fair future, for neurodiversity, and for the planet is the same fight as that for a Free Palestine. No one is free until the occupation ends.
"They call us crazy because we cannot remain silent. But when the whole world is lying, telling the truth is a revolutionary act."

We refuse to bow any longer to a system that puts profit above life. We demand an end to the lies. We demand a future for ALL children — from Stockholm to Gaza.
