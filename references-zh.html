全球机构与数据集

  • WHO & UNICEF: State of the World's Children and Mental Health Atlases for data on adolescent depression and anxiety.
  • SIPRI (Stockholm International Peace Research Institute): Annual reports on global military expenditure and the arms trade.
  • Oxfam International: "Survival of the Richest" and annual inequality reports for wealth distribution and tax avoidance data.
  • World Bank & FAO: Global poverty lines and the State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World.
  • Basel Action Network (BAN): Tracking of global e-waste flows and waste trade violations.
  • IMF & Bank of England: Historical data on fiat money and reports on modern money creation.

法律与人权来源

  • United Nations: 1948 Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide.
  • ICJ (International Court of Justice): Official filings and rulings on state accountability and humanitarian law.
  • Amnesty International & Human Rights Watch: Investigative reports on systemic apartheid, digital surveillance, and environmental destruction.
