Blood Money: The Industry of Death

While the world watches the destruction of Gaza and other regions in horror, the global arms industry sees a business opportunity. The 1% profit directly from the production and sale of the machinery used to displace and kill.

Global Arms Trade Facts

  • Record Profits: Global military expenditure reached a record high of over $2.2 trillion in recent years. The top five arms-producing companies are all based in the West, consistently reporting multi-billion dollar profits.
  • Fueling Occupation: Billions of dollars in military aid and weapons sales flow annually to the state of Israel, enabling the ongoing occupation and the destruction of civilian infrastructure.
  • The Taxpayer Connection: Much of this industry is funded by public money. Governments prioritize defense contracts over social welfare, effectively using taxpayer funds to subsidize the 1%'s profit from war.
  • Exports of Destruction: Major global powers continue to export advanced weaponry to regions of conflict, prioritizing economic gain over human rights and international law.
"War is a racket. It is the only one in which the profits are reckoned in dollars and the losses in lives." — Smedley Butler
Sources: SIPRI (Stockholm International Peace Research Institute), Amnesty International, Campaign Against Arms Trade (CAAT).
