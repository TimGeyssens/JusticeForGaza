Essential Cinema

  • Das Experiment (2001): Thriller based on the "Stanford Experiment," vividly showing how authority corrupts and dehumanizes.
  • Children of Men: A stark vision of a world without a future, mirroring our "Ecocide" and "Extraction" machine.
  • Snowpiercer: A literal train-based hierarchy where the 1% live in luxury while the rest suffer in the back.
  • V for Vendetta: An exploration of resistance, "Propaganda," and the power of an individual to spark a collective movement.
  • Dark Waters (2019): A lawyer takes on a corporate giant responsible for global chemical contamination, a stark look at "Greenwashing" and ecocide.

Critical Documentaries

  • Dopesick (2021): While a series, this is an essential look at the "Symptom Trap" and "Medical Apartheid" where the 1% profit from engineered addiction.
  • Inside Job: A detailed look at the 2008 financial crisis and the "Evolution of Money."
  • The True Cost: An investigation into the "Modern Slavery" and "Waste Crisis" caused by global fashion.
  • Human Flow: Ai Weiwei's documentary on the global refugee crisis and displacement.
  • Born in Gaza: A raw look at the lives of children in Gaza and the impact of the ongoing occupation.
