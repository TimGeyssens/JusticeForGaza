The Strategy of Disunity

The 1% system is fragile. It only survives because the 99% are kept in a state of constant internal conflict. By weaponizing our identities—nationalism, religion, and even sports—the system ensures that we view each other as enemies rather than the structures that exploit us all.

Artificial Enmities

  • Nationalism: Flags are used to create an "us vs. them" mentality, making it easier to justify the No Human is Illegal arms trade and ecocide in the name of "national interest."
  • Religious Conflict: Spiritual beliefs are often manipulated by the elite to fuel wars and internal division, obscuring the shared economic and human struggles that unite us.
  • Sports & Tribalism: Modern sports act as the ultimate "circenses." They channel our natural tribal instincts into harmless competition over games, draining the energy needed for political and social revolt.

The Outcome of Division

While we argue over borders, gods, or jerseys, the 1% continue to accumulate wealth and power. They do not care about your religion or your team; they only care that you are too busy fighting over them to notice the "Tax Avoidance" or the "Waste Crisis" that affects everyone's survival.

"If the 99% ever realized they have more in common with each other than with the elite, the system would collapse by tomorrow morning. Disunity is their only defense."
