Collective Intelligence

This manifesto is a living resource. To truly counter the 1% system, we must pool our knowledge, statistics, and perspectives. If you have an idea for a new page, a powerful video, or data that needs to be brought to light, we want to hear from you.

How to Contribute

  • GitHub Issues: The best way to suggest new content is by opening an issue on our GitHub repository. You can describe your idea or provide links to resources.
  • Pull Requests: If you are technically inclined, you can fork the repository, add your content yourself, and submit a Pull Request. This ensures the site remains decentralized and resilient.
  • Share Resources: We are looking for data on systemic extraction, personal stories of resistance, and educational videos that bypass corporate media filters.
