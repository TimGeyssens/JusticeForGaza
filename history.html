Territorial Erasure

The history of Palestine over the last century is one of systematic displacement and territorial shrinkage. From the British Mandate to the present day, the land controlled by Palestinians has been reduced to fragmented enclaves.

Visualizing the History

The Impact of Displacement

This territorial shrinkage is not just about lines on a map. It represents the destruction of communities, the loss of agricultural heritage, and a systematic denial of the right to self-determination. The ongoing expansion of settlements continues to erode the possibility of a viable Palestinian state.

"To understand the present crisis, one must see the map. It is the story of a people being slowly pushed out of their own home."
