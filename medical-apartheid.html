Life Expectancy Gap

The gap in life expectancy between the West and the Global South is not a natural occurrence; it is the result of systematic extraction. In the 1% system, the right to health is replaced by the ability to pay. While preventable diseases are eradicated in wealthy nations, they are allowed to persist and even return as weapons in occupied territories.

Statistics of Exclusion

  • Access to Vaccines: During global health crises, wealthy nations consistently hoard vaccines, leaving poor countries with less than 10% coverage for months or years.
  • Patent Profit: Pharmaceutical companies prioritize patent protection over human lives, preventing the production of affordable generic medicines for HIV, cancer, and infectious diseases in the Global South.
  • Weaponized Disease: In Gaza, the systematic destruction of healthcare infrastructure has led to the return of diseases like Polio and mass outbreaks of preventable skin and respiratory infections.
