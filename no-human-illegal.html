The Desire for a Better Life

Migration is as old as humanity itself. It is the fundamental right to seek safety, dignity, and a better future for oneself and one's family. In the 1% system, migration is framed as a "crisis" or a "threat," but the real crisis is the systemic inequality that forces people to leave their homes in the first place.

The Hypocrisy of Borders

The global extraction machine relies on the free flow of capital, resources, and weapons across borders. Yet, when human beings—often fleeing the very "Ecocide," "Modern Slavery," and "Arms Trade" fueled by the 1%—try to move, they are met with walls, drones, and the dehumanizing label of "illegal." Borders are not designed to protect people; they are designed to protect property and maintain the "Wealth Gap."

The Dehumanization Machine

  • Manufacturing Fear: As discussed in the "Information War," corporate media uses migration to fuel "Divide & Conquer" narratives, distracting the working class from the real villains by blaming those with the least power.
  • The Criminalization of Survival: Labeling a person as "illegal" is a biological and moral impossibility. It is a legal fiction used to justify the "Prison Industrial" complex and the denial of "Medical Apartheid" level care to those in need.
  • Climate Displacement: As the climate crisis accelerates, millions more will be forced into migration. "Climate Justice" is impossible without the recognition that no human being is illegal on a dying planet.
"No human being is illegal. The only thing that is illegal is a system that extracts life from the Global South and then builds a wall to keep the victims out."
