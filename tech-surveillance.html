Digital Panopticon

Modern warfare and occupation have been revolutionized by technology. Data is no longer just information; it is a weapon. In the 21st century, the 1% use Artificial Intelligence, biometric surveillance, and mass data harvesting to automate oppression and eliminate the human element from the "extraction machine."

AI as an Executioner

Recent reports have exposed the use of AI systems, such as 'Lavender' and 'Where's Daddy?', to generate target lists in Gaza. These systems prioritize efficiency and quantity over accuracy, leading to mass civilian casualties. This is the ultimate "Neuro-typical" logic—a cold, mathematical optimization of destruction that ignores the rawness of human life.

Surveillance Capitalism

  • Biometric Control: In the West Bank, facial recognition technology is used at every checkpoint, creating a digital cage for an entire population. This technology is then exported globally as "field-tested" security solutions.
  • Data Extraction: Big Tech companies collaborate with military and intelligence agencies, providing the infrastructure for mass surveillance. Your data is the fuel for this machine.
  • Algorithmic Erasure: Online platforms use automated moderation to shadowban and remove content that challenges state-sponsored narratives, effectively silencing the unfiltered truth.
"When war is automated by algorithms, accountability vanishes into the code. We must refuse to be reduced to data points in a killing machine."
